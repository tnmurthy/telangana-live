# RTI, Courts & Legal Help

This section helps citizens understand their legal rights and how to use them: from filing an RTI to checking a court case online, accessing Lok Adalat for quick settlements, and getting free legal help if you cannot afford a lawyer.

> **This is not an official government website.** All applications must be made on official portals. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [RTI Application](./rti-application) | Ask any government department for information or records |
| [Court Case Status](./court-case-status) | Check next hearing date and case details for any court |
| [Lok Adalat](./lok-adalat) | Settle disputes quickly and free through Lok Adalat |
| [Free Legal Aid](./free-legal-aid) | Get a free government-appointed lawyer if you cannot afford one |

## Key facts

- RTI applications must be responded to within **30 days** (or 48 hours for life/liberty matters).
- Free legal aid is available to anyone below the poverty line, women, children, senior citizens, and persons with disabilities — **by law**.
- Lok Adalat settlements cannot be appealed and have the force of a court decree.

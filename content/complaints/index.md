# Complaints & Grievances

If a government service is delayed, denied, or not working properly, you have the right to raise a formal complaint. Telangana has several official grievance channels — from the state-level Prajavani to the national PG Portal — and each department has its own complaint mechanism.

> **This is not an official government website.** All complaints must be filed on official portals. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [Prajavani](./prajavani) | File a complaint about any Telangana government department |
| [GHMC Complaints](./ghmc-complaints) | Report civic issues (potholes, garbage, streetlights) in Hyderabad |
| [PG Portal](./pg-portal) | National grievance portal for central and state departments |
| [Road & Civic Issues](./road-civic-issues) | Report road damage and civic problems outside Hyderabad |

## Tips for effective complaints

- Be specific: mention dates, names of officials (if known), application numbers, and what exactly went wrong.
- Attach evidence: a photograph, a copy of your application, or the rejection letter.
- Keep your complaint reference number — you will need it to track and escalate.
- If a complaint on one portal is not resolved, escalate to Prajavani or the PG Portal.

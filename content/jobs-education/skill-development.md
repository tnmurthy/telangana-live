# Skill Development & PMKVY

Skill development programmes in Telangana offer free or subsidised vocational training in trades like electrician, plumber, welder, beautician, IT/computer operator, retail, and many more. The national Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) and the state's Telangana Academy for Skill and Knowledge (TASK) are the main programmes.

## Who should use this

- Young people aged 15–35 who want to learn a trade and improve their employment chances
- Students who dropped out of formal education but want a recognised skill certificate
- Unemployed youth looking for practical training in high-demand sectors
- Employers or self-employed people who want to upskill and get a formal certification

## Steps in short

1. Visit the PMKVY official website or TASK portal (links below) and search for skill courses available near you.
2. Select the sector (manufacturing, services, IT, healthcare, etc.) and the specific job role you want to train in.
3. Locate a government-empanelled training centre in your area or district.
4. Register online or directly at the training centre with your Aadhaar card and educational certificates.
5. Complete the training programme (duration varies: 2 weeks to 6 months depending on the course).
6. Appear for the skill assessment test conducted by an authorised assessment body.
7. Download or collect your skill certificate after passing; this certificate is recognised by employers across India.

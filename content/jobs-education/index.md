# Jobs, Education & Scholarships

Telangana offers significant support for students and job seekers — from fully funded scholarships for SC/ST/BC/Minority students to government recruitment through TSPSC and free vocational skill training. This section helps you find and apply for the right opportunity.

> **This is not an official government website.** All applications happen on official portals. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [TSPSC Government Jobs](./tspsc-jobs) | Apply for Telangana state government jobs via TSPSC |
| [Pre-Matric Scholarship](./pre-matric-scholarship) | Fee support for SC/ST/BC/Minority students in Classes 1–10 |
| [Post-Matric Scholarship](./post-matric-scholarship) | Fee reimbursement for SC/ST/BC/Minority students in college |
| [Skill Development & PMKVY](./skill-development) | Free vocational training with a recognised national certificate |

## Key portals

- **TSPSC** – Telangana state government job recruitment
- **Telangana ePass** – state scholarship applications (pre and post-matric)
- **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** – central government scholarships
- **TASK / PMKVY** – free skill training and certification

# Police & Safety

Telangana Police offers several online services so citizens can file complaints, get clearance certificates, and access safety resources without always visiting a police station. This section covers the most commonly used police-related services.

> **This is not an official government website.** All complaints and applications must be made on official police portals. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [Online FIR / Complaint](./online-fir) | File a theft, cybercrime, or harassment complaint online |
| [Character Certificate](./character-certificate) | Get a police clearance certificate for passport, jobs, or visa |
| [Tenant & Servant Verification](./tenant-verification) | Register tenants or domestic workers with the local police |
| [Women Safety – SHe Teams](./women-safety) | Report harassment or violence; get emergency help |

## Emergency numbers

- **112** – All emergencies (police, fire, ambulance)
- **100** – Police helpline
- **181** – Women's helpline (24 hours, free)
- **1930** – Cybercrime helpline

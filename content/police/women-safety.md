# Women Safety – SHe Teams & Helplines

Telangana Police runs dedicated SHe Teams that respond to harassment, stalking, and domestic violence complaints from women. There are also 24-hour helplines and online complaint options so that any woman in distress can get help quickly, any time of day.

## Who should use this

- Women who are being harassed, stalked, or threatened in public or online
- Domestic violence survivors who need immediate help or want to file a complaint
- Parents or relatives of a woman in distress who want to raise a complaint on her behalf
- Women who want to report workplace sexual harassment (POSH Act) and need guidance on how to proceed

## Steps in short

1. In an emergency, call **100** (Police) or **181** (Women's Helpline, Telangana) immediately. SHe Teams are mobile and can reach you quickly.
2. For a non-urgent complaint, visit the Telangana Police online complaint portal or the Bharosa Centre nearest to you.
3. Describe the incident: what happened, where, when, and details of the person responsible.
4. Attach any evidence — screenshots of messages, photographs, or medical records — if filing online.
5. Submit the complaint. A SHe Team officer will follow up within the prescribed response time.
6. For domestic violence cases, a Protection Officer can also be reached through the Women's Helpline.
7. All complaints are handled with confidentiality.

# Telangana.live – Portal Sitemap

> **Note:** This is not an official government website. Telangana.live explains and deep-links to official Telangana government services (MeeSeva, Prajavani, TS Police, GHMC, CEO Telangana, etc.). All actual applications, payments, and transactions must be completed on official government portals.

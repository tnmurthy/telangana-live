# Caste Certificate

A caste certificate is an official document confirming that you belong to a Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), or Other Backward Class (OBC) as recognised by the Telangana government. It is required for reservations in jobs, education, and government schemes.

## Who should use this

- Students seeking reserved seats or fee waivers in colleges and universities
- Job applicants competing for government posts under SC/ST/OBC quota
- Families applying for caste-specific welfare schemes
- First-time applicants who have never obtained a caste certificate before

## Steps in short

1. Visit the MeeSeva portal and log in (or register if new).
2. Search for "Caste Certificate" and click **Apply**.
3. Select your caste category (SC / ST / BC-A / BC-B / BC-C / BC-D / BC-E) and enter personal details.
4. Upload documents: Aadhaar card, ration card, and a proof of caste (parent's caste certificate or school records).
5. Pay the service fee online.
6. Save your Application Reference Number.
7. The Revenue / Tahsildar office verifies the application; download the certificate once approved (7–21 working days).

# Documents & Certificates

Getting an official certificate in Telangana is easier than ever — most documents can be applied for online through MeeSeva or at a nearby MeeSeva centre. This section covers the most commonly needed certificates, explains what each one is for, and shows you the steps to apply.

> **This is not an official government website.** All applications happen on official portals. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Available Certificates

| Certificate | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [Income Certificate](./income-certificate) | Proof of family income for scholarships, BPL benefits, and fee waivers |
| [Caste Certificate](./caste-certificate) | Proof of SC/ST/BC category for reservations in jobs and education |
| [Residence Certificate](./residence-certificate) | Proof of Telangana domicile for state quota seats and jobs |
| [Birth Certificate](./birth-certificate) | Official record of birth — needed for school, passport, voting |
| [Death Certificate](./death-certificate) | Official record of death — needed for inheritance, insurance, pension |
| [EWS Certificate](./ews-certificate) | Economically Weaker Section certificate for 10% general-category reservation |
| [Family Membership Certificate](./family-membership-certificate) | Lists all family members — needed for inheritance, pension, and welfare |

## General flow for MeeSeva certificates

1. Visit the MeeSeva portal or a MeeSeva centre.
2. Apply with Aadhaar + supporting documents.
3. Pay the small service fee (₹35–₹60 for most certificates).
4. Track status with your reference number.
5. Download and print the certificate once approved.

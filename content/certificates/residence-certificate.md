# Residence / Domicile Certificate

A residence certificate (also called a domicile certificate) is proof that you live in Telangana. Many government jobs, university admissions, and state welfare schemes require you to be a resident of Telangana for a certain number of years.

## Who should use this

- Students applying for state-quota seats in engineering, medical, or other colleges
- Job seekers applying for state government jobs that require Telangana domicile
- Citizens needing proof of address for other official purposes
- Residents who recently moved and need to establish a new domicile

## Steps in short

1. Log in to the MeeSeva portal and search for "Residence Certificate" or "Domicile Certificate."
2. Click **Apply** and fill in your name, address, and how long you have lived at that address.
3. Upload supporting documents: Aadhaar card, ration card, and any utility bill showing your current address.
4. Pay the service fee online.
5. Save the Application Reference Number.
6. The local Tahsildar office reviews and approves the application.
7. Download and print the certificate once approved (usually 7–15 working days).

# Bills & Taxes

You can pay most utility bills and government taxes in Telangana online — from your phone — without visiting any office or standing in a queue. This section covers electricity, water, property tax, traffic challans, and vehicle-related payments.

> **This is not an official government website.** All payments happen on official portals. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [Electricity Bill Payment](./electricity-bill-payment) | Pay TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL bill online |
| [Water Bill Payment](./water-bill-payment) | Pay HMWSSB or municipal water bill |
| [Property Tax](./property-tax) | Pay annual property tax to GHMC or local municipality |
| [Traffic Challan](./traffic-challan) | Check and pay pending traffic fines |
| [RTA / Vehicle Tax & Fees](./rta-vehicle-tax) | Pay road tax, renew RC, renew driving licence |

## Tips for online payments

- Keep your Consumer Number / Connection ID / Vehicle Registration Number handy before you start.
- Always save the payment receipt (download the PDF or note the transaction ID).
- If you pay and the system shows an error, wait 24 hours before paying again — the first payment may still go through.

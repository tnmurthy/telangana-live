# Water Bill Payment

This page explains how to pay your water and sewerage bill online in Telangana. For Hyderabad, the Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply & Sewerage Board (HMWSSB) manages the connections. Other cities and towns have their own municipal water departments.

## Who should use this

- Households in Hyderabad and other Telangana towns with a HMWSSB or municipal water connection
- Apartment building managers paying bulk water charges
- Citizens who want to check previous billing statements or payment history
- Anyone whose water supply was disconnected due to non-payment

## Steps in short

1. Visit the HMWSSB official website or the relevant municipal portal for your town.
2. Enter your Consumer Number (printed on your water bill or welcome letter).
3. Your current outstanding amount will appear.
4. Verify the amount and click **Pay Now**.
5. Choose a payment method: UPI, net banking, or debit/credit card.
6. Save the payment receipt with the transaction reference number.
7. Water supply is restored or continued within 24 hours after a late payment is cleared.

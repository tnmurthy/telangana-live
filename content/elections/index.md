# Elections & Voting

Every registered voter in Telangana has the right to participate in local body, state assembly, and national elections. This section helps you register, check your name in the voter list, find your polling booth, and update your details — all online.

> **This is not an official government website.** All voter services are managed by the Election Commission of India and CEO Telangana. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [Register as a Voter](./voter-registration) | Add your name to the electoral roll for the first time |
| [Check Voter List](./check-voter-list) | Verify your name is in the current electoral roll |
| [Find Polling Booth](./polling-booth) | Get your assigned polling station address and room number |
| [Update Address](./address-update) | Correct address or transfer registration to a new constituency |

## Key facts

- Any Indian citizen aged **18 or above** on the qualifying date is eligible to vote.
- Voter registration (adding or updating) is free and can be done entirely online.
- Call the **Voter Helpline 1950** (free) for any election-related query.

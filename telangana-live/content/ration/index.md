# Ration, Food & Pensions

This section covers two essential services for low-income families in Telangana: the Public Distribution System (PDS) for subsidised food, and the Aasara social security scheme for monthly pensions to senior citizens, widows, and persons with disabilities.

> **This is not an official government website.** All applications happen through official channels. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [New Ration Card](./new-ration-card) | Apply for a ration card to buy subsidised rice and other essentials |
| [Update Ration Card](./ration-card-update) | Add/remove members, correct name or address on your ration card |
| [Old Age Pension (Aasara)](./old-age-pension) | Monthly pension for senior citizens with no regular income |
| [Widow & Disability Pension](./widow-disability-pension) | Monthly pension for widows and persons with disabilities |
| [Aasara Scheme Overview](./aasara-pension) | All Aasara pension categories and how to apply |

## How to apply for most services here

Most ration and pension services in Telangana start at the **Ward Secretariat** (urban) or **Village Secretariat** (rural). You can also apply online through the Civil Supplies portal (ration card) or the Social Welfare portal (pensions).

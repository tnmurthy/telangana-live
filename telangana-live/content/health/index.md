# Health & Social Welfare

Telangana provides free hospitalisation insurance, neighbourhood health clinics, disability support, and a wide range of welfare schemes for marginalised communities. This section helps you find and access the health and welfare services you are entitled to.

> **This is not an official government website.** All applications happen on official portals or at government offices. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [Aarogyasri / PM-JAY](./aarogyasri) | Free hospitalisation up to ₹5 lakh per year for eligible families |
| [Basthi Dawakhana](./basthi-dawakhana) | Free doctor consultation and medicines at urban health clinics |
| [Disability Certificate](./disability-certificate) | Official certification of disability for pensions and reservations |
| [Welfare Schemes Overview](./welfare-schemes) | Find schemes for SC/ST/BC, women, children, and other groups |

## Emergency health contacts

- **108** – Emergency ambulance (free, 24 hours)
- **104** – Medical advice helpline (free, 24 hours)
- **14410** – Aarogyasri / PM-JAY helpline

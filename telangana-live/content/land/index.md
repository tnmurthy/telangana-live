# Land & Property

Telangana has moved most land and property services online — from checking who owns a piece of land to registering a property sale. This section explains the most common land and property tasks and tells you which portal to use.

> **This is not an official government website.** All applications and registrations happen on official portals. We explain the process and link you to the right place.

## Services in this section

| Service | What you can do |
|---|---|
| [Land Records – Dharani](./land-records-dharani) | View and download agricultural land records (RoR / passbook) |
| [Encumbrance Certificate (EC)](./encumbrance-certificate) | Check if a property has any existing loans or legal disputes |
| [Property Registration](./property-registration) | Register a sale deed, gift deed, or mortgage |
| [Mutation / Patta Transfer](./mutation-patta-transfer) | Update land records to the new owner's name |
| [Building Permissions](./building-permissions) | Get permission to construct or extend a building |

## Key portals for land and property

- **Dharani** – for agricultural land records across Telangana
- **IGRS Telangana** (Registration & Stamps) – for property registration and encumbrance certificates
- **TG-bPASS** – for building permissions and occupancy certificates
- **MeeSeva** – for encumbrance certificates and certified document copies

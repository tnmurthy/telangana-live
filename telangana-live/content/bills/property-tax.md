# Property Tax Payment

Property tax is a yearly charge on buildings and land that you own. In Telangana, GHMC collects it in Hyderabad; other towns and villages have their own municipal bodies. Paying on time keeps you compliant and avoids penalties.

## Who should use this

- Homeowners paying annual property tax on their house or plot
- Business owners with commercial properties registered in a Telangana urban area
- Citizens who want to check their property tax dues or get a no-dues certificate
- Landlords managing multiple properties across municipal limits

## Steps in short

1. Visit the GHMC website (for Hyderabad) or your town's municipal portal.
2. Click **Property Tax** and enter your PTIN (Property Tax Identification Number) — found on any previous tax receipt.
3. If you don't have a PTIN, search by owner name, door number, or locality.
4. Your annual tax demand and any arrears or penalties will appear.
5. Verify the amount and click **Pay Online**.
6. Complete payment using UPI, net banking, or card.
7. Download the tax paid receipt — you may need it for property sale, loan, or building permission applications.

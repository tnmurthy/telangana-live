# Growth & Outreach Plan - 30 Days

This plan is designed to seed the initial audience for **Telangana.live** through local, high-leverage distribution channels.

## Week 1: Foundational Distribution & WhatsApp
**Goal:** Reach local hyper-connected groups.
- **WhatsApp Broadcast Lists:** Create a WhatsApp Business account. Set up automated daily broadcasts for "Gold Rates & Petrol Prices" (highly shared content in India).
- **Local Groups:** Share deep links to specific utilities (e.g., `telangana.live/reservoirs` in farmer/agriculture groups).
- **Google News:** Submit the portal to Google Publisher Center as a local news aggregator to get indexed in the "News" tab.

## Week 2: Telegram & Twitter/X
**Goal:** Capture the tech-savvy and real-time news audience.
- **Telegram Channel:** Create "Telangana Live Alerts". Post automated 3x daily updates (Morning Weather, Afternoon News, Evening Fuel/Gold). Pin a link to the web app.
- **Twitter/X Bot:** Create an automated bot that tweets out real-time reservoir levels and major news headlines using hashtags like `#Telangana #Hyderabad #TGNews`.

## Week 3: Local Press & PR
**Goal:** Get backlinks from established local media.
- **Press Release:** Send a PR to local digital outlets (Way2News, Telugu360, GreatAndhra).
  - *Headline Hook:* "New Civic Portal Brings Real-Time Telangana Government Data, Fuel, and News to One Ad-Free Dashboard."
  - *Focus:* Emphasize the ad-free, data-driven aspect of the portal.

## Week 4: SEO Optimization & Organic Growth
**Goal:** Solidify organic search rankings.
- Ensure all dynamic routes have SSR (Server-Side Rendering) or prerendering (via Vite's SSG capabilities) so Googlebot can read the dynamic content.
- Target long-tail keywords: "today petrol price in hyderabad 2026", "srisailam reservoir water level live".

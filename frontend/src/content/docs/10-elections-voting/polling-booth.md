# Find Your Polling Booth

Your polling booth is the specific location where you must go to cast your vote. The booth assigned to you depends on your registered address. You can find your booth address and booth number online before election day so you are not searching on the day itself.

## Who should use this

- All registered voters preparing for an upcoming Telangana or national election
- First-time voters who have never been to their assigned polling booth
- Citizens who registered at a new address and are unsure which booth they belong to
- Voters who want to confirm the booth address, room number, and polling officer details

## Steps in short

1. Visit the Voter Helpline Portal (voters.eci.gov.in) or the CEO Telangana portal.
2. Search for your name in the electoral roll using your EPIC number or personal details (see Check Voter List page).
3. Your voter slip details will show your **Part Number**, **Serial Number**, and **Polling Station Name and Address**.
4. Note down the booth address; you can also get a printed voter slip from the portal.
5. On election day, carry your Voter ID card (EPIC) or any approved alternative photo ID (Aadhaar, driving licence, passport, PAN card, etc.).
6. At the booth, tell the polling officer your serial number. They will mark your name and give you a ballot.
7. Vote, ink your finger, and collect your voting confirmation slip.

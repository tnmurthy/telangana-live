# Prajavani Grievance Portal

Prajavani is the Telangana government's official public grievance platform where citizens can submit complaints about government services or departments. Your complaint is assigned to the relevant officer, who must respond or resolve it within a set number of days.

## Who should use this

- Citizens whose government service application (certificate, pension, ration card) is stuck or delayed
- Anyone who was denied a government service without a valid reason
- People who faced misbehaviour or negligence from a government department
- Citizens following up on a complaint they filed at a government office that was never resolved

## Steps in short

1. Visit the Prajavani portal or the Telangana CMO Prajavani page (link below).
2. Click **Submit Grievance** and log in with your mobile number and OTP.
3. Select the department or service your complaint is related to.
4. Describe the problem in clear, simple language: what happened, when, and what you need.
5. Attach any supporting documents (rejected letter, application copy, photographs) if available.
6. Submit and note your Grievance Reference Number.
7. Track the status online; the concerned officer must reply within the prescribed time limit.

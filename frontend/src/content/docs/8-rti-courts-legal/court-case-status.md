# Court Case Status Check

You can check the status of any civil or criminal case in Telangana's district courts, the Telangana High Court, and the Supreme Court of India using free online portals. You do not need a lawyer to check a case's next hearing date or order.

## Who should use this

- Parties to a civil or criminal case who want to know the next hearing date
- Lawyers and legal assistants managing multiple cases across courts
- Citizens who filed a complaint and want to know if a case was registered
- Families of individuals in court proceedings who want to track case progress

## Steps in short

1. Visit the eCourts portal (ecourts.gov.in) for district court cases across Telangana.
2. Click **Case Status** and select Telangana state and the specific district court.
3. Search using case number, party name (plaintiff or defendant), advocate name, or FIR number.
4. The case details — filing date, next hearing date, and last order — will appear.
5. For Telangana High Court cases, use the High Court's own case status portal (link below).
6. For Supreme Court cases, use the Supreme Court of India website.
7. You can opt to receive SMS alerts for future hearing dates on the eCourts portal.

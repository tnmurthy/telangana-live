# TSPSC Government Jobs

The Telangana State Public Service Commission (TSPSC) is the body that recruits candidates for most state government jobs in Telangana — from Group-I officers to Group-IV clerical posts. All recruitment notifications, applications, and results are published on the official TSPSC website.

## Who should use this

- Job seekers who want to apply for Telangana state government positions
- Candidates who have already applied and want to check exam dates or results
- Students in their final year of college who want to prepare for upcoming TSPSC exams
- Anyone who wants to download their hall ticket or check cut-off marks

## Steps in short

1. Visit the official TSPSC website and check the **Notifications** section for open recruitments.
2. Read the notification carefully: eligibility (age, qualification, caste), the number of posts, and last date to apply.
3. Register on the TSPSC portal with your mobile number and create a profile.
4. Fill in the online application form for the specific recruitment and upload your photograph and signature.
5. Pay the application fee online (fee is waived for SC/ST and other exempt categories — check the notification).
6. Download and print the application confirmation and keep it safe.
7. Attend the exam on the date mentioned; download your hall ticket from the same portal a week before the exam.

# Pre-Matric Scholarship

The pre-matric scholarship supports students studying in Classes 1 to 10 from SC, ST, BC, EBC, and minority communities. It covers a portion of tuition fees, maintenance, and other educational costs so that financial hardship does not force students out of school.

## Who should use this

- Parents of SC/ST/BC/Minority students studying in Classes 1–10 who need fee support
- Hostel-going students in government welfare schools who want to claim maintenance allowance
- First-generation learners whose families have no prior scholarship history
- Students who applied last year and need to renew their scholarship for the current academic year

## Steps in short

1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or the Telangana ePass scholarship portal (link below).
2. Register with the student's Aadhaar number and create a login.
3. Select the correct scholarship scheme based on category (SC, ST, BC, Minority, EBC) and school type.
4. Fill in the application: student details, school details, bank account (in student's or parent's name), and family income details.
5. Upload documents: Aadhaar, caste certificate, income certificate, fee receipt from the school, and bank passbook.
6. Submit the application before the last date (usually September–October each year).
7. Track the status online; the scholarship amount is transferred directly to the bank account.

# Post-Matric Scholarship

The post-matric scholarship covers SC, ST, BC, EBC, and minority students who are studying after Class 10 — in Intermediate (Classes 11–12), degree, diploma, engineering, medical, or other professional courses. It can cover full or partial tuition fees and a maintenance allowance.

## Who should use this

- SC/ST/BC/Minority students studying in Intermediate, degree, or professional colleges
- Students from low-income families who cannot afford private college tuition fees
- Students renewing their scholarship for the current academic year
- Students who joined a new college and need to update their scholarship details

## Steps in short

1. Visit the Telangana ePass portal or the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).
2. Register with the student's Aadhaar number or log in if you already have an account.
3. Select the post-matric scholarship category that matches your community and course.
4. Fill in the application: personal details, college/institution name, course, year of study, and family income.
5. Upload documents: Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, admission fee receipt, and bank passbook.
6. Submit the application before the deadline (usually September–November each year).
7. The scholarship is directly transferred to the student's bank account after college verification and government approval.

# Electricity Bill Payment

This page shows you how to pay your Telangana electricity bill online. You can pay through the official TSSPDCL (Southern districts) or TSNPDCL (Northern districts) websites, or through MeeSeva, so you never need to stand in a queue.

## Who should use this

- Households wanting to pay their monthly electricity bill from a phone or computer
- Business owners managing commercial connection bills
- Anyone who has received a disconnection notice and needs to pay urgently
- Citizens who want to check their current meter readings or billing history

## Steps in short

1. Find out which discom covers your area – TSSPDCL (Hyderabad and southern Telangana) or TSNPDCL (northern Telangana).
2. Visit the correct official website (links below) or open the MeeSeva portal.
3. Enter your 11-digit Service Connection Number (printed on your bill) and your registered mobile number.
4. Your outstanding bill will appear on screen. Verify the amount.
5. Choose a payment method: UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking.
6. Complete the payment and save the receipt (transaction ID).
7. The receipt is your proof of payment; a revised bill or reconnection happens within 24 hours if disconnected.

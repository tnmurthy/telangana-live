# Income Certificate

An income certificate is an official document that states your family's annual income. Government schools, colleges, and welfare schemes use it to decide whether you qualify for fee waivers, reservations, or scholarships.

## Who should use this

- Students applying for fee reimbursement or scholarships
- Families applying for government housing schemes (PMAY, EWS plots)
- Anyone needing to prove below-poverty-line (BPL) status for welfare benefits
- Citizens applying for caste-based reservations that also need an income proof

## Steps in short

1. Visit the MeeSeva portal (link below) and create or log in to your account.
2. Search for "Income Certificate" and click **Apply**.
3. Fill in your family details: names, occupation, and estimated annual income.
4. Upload supporting documents: Aadhaar card, ration card, last salary slip or self-declaration of income.
5. Pay the small service fee (usually ₹35–₹45) online.
6. Note your Application Reference Number.
7. Track the status online; download and print the certificate once approved (typically within 7–15 working days).

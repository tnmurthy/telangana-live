# Character / Police Clearance Certificate

A Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) or Character Certificate confirms that you do not have a criminal record in Telangana. It is commonly required for passport applications, overseas employment, higher education abroad, and visa applications.

## Who should use this

- Citizens applying for a passport who need a PCC at the regional passport office
- People seeking jobs abroad or applying for overseas employment visas
- Students applying for higher education outside India who need a character certificate
- Employees in sensitive sectors (finance, education, security) required to submit a background check

## Steps in short

1. Visit the Telangana Police official website or MeeSeva and select **Police Clearance Certificate**.
2. Register or log in with your mobile number.
3. Fill in your personal details: full name, date of birth, Aadhaar number, current and permanent address.
4. Mention the purpose (passport, employment, education) as required.
5. Upload documents: Aadhaar card, passport copy (if available), address proof, and a passport-size photograph.
6. Pay the prescribed fee online and note your application number.
7. The local police station conducts a background check; download or collect the certificate once cleared (usually 7–21 working days).
